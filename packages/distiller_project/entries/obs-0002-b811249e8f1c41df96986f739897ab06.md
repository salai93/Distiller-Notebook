## OA review: "Consume Safe Drinking Water" operational process

**Model:** Distiller-Project @ master, commit 973e572
**Element:** Consume Safe Drinking Water (OperationalProcess, uuid 1d37dd5c-b9e0-4178-9663-764352c1f152)

### Structure
8 operational activities linked by 8 functional exchanges:
- Operate System → WaterControl → Provide Dirty Water; Operate System → PowerControl → Provide Operating Power
- Provide Dirty Water → DirtyWater → Purify Water; Provide Operating Power → OperatingPower → Purify Water
- Purify Water → Waste → Manage Waste
- Purify Water → SafeDrinkingWater3 → Store Water → SafeDrinkingWater2 → Distribute Water → SafeDrinkingWater1 → Use Water

### Requirement traceability (Satisfy)
- Provide Operating Power ← MR.1 Electrical Power (REQ-001189), MR.2 Sources of Heat (REQ-001190)
- Operate System ← MR.4.1 Intuitive Operation (REQ-001193), MR.8 Local Operators (REQ-001208)
- Provide Dirty Water ← MR.3 Sources of Water (REQ-001191)
- Distribute Water ← MR.5 Clean Water Distribution (REQ-001194)
- Manage Waste ← MR.6 Waste Management (REQ-001206)
- Process as a whole ← Water Distribution Test (REQ-001211)

### Gaps found
1. **No requirement traceability on Purify Water, Store Water, or Use Water.** Purify Water is the core transformation, so this is the most significant gap.
2. **Exchange items are allocated on only 2 of 8 exchanges:** DirtyWater (carries DirtyWater) and SafeDrinkingWater1 (carries SafeWater). SafeDrinkingWater2/3, OperatingPower, PowerControl, WaterControl and Waste carry none.
3. **No activity decomposition.** All activities sit directly under Root Operational Activity.
4. **No descriptions** on any of the activities or exchanges.
5. **Naming:** the safe-water exchanges are numbered against the flow direction (3 → 2 → 1).
