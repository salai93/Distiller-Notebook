## Impact of REQ-001211 "Water Distribution Test" on the Physical Architecture

**Model:** Distiller-Project @ master 1350169, traced with realized and realizing links included.

### Correction to OBS-0002 and OBS-0003
Those entries said the OA process "Consume Safe Drinking Water" has no realizing functional chain. **That was wrong.** The earlier session had been opened without cross-layer links. The process is realized by a full chain:

- OA process "Consume Safe Drinking Water" (1d37dd5c)
- realized by SA "Distill Water" (b3196a9c)
- realized by LA "Distill Water" (987d9f65)
- realized by PA "Distill Water" (4725381b)

REQ-001211 therefore has a direct trace to the Physical Architecture through this chain.

### The requirement
REQ-001211/A satisfies only the OA process. Its text is just the title "Water Distribution Test" plus an image embedded from Teamcenter, with no readable shall statement. Exactly what changes depends on what that test requires.

### What the PA chain "Distill Water" involves
Functions inside the Water Distiller:
- Heat Water and Condense Steam → Condenser
- Boil Water → Evaporator (Mechanical Boiler)
- Divert Flow → Splitter (Tee Fitting)
- Throttle → Feed (Feed Valve)
- Drain Residue → Drain (Drain Valve)

Functions on the external actors: Generate Heat, Operate System, Provide Dirty Water, Store Clean Water, Distribute Clean Water, Use Clean Water, Discharge Residue and Discharge Excess Water. The PA model check confirms every function is allocated. Which actor each one sits on is inferred from the names, not read directly.

Exchanges in the chain and the component exchange / physical link that carries each:
- DirtyWater2: Pipeline / SourcePipeline
- Heat3: HeatSupply / Heat
- Steam: Steam / SteamPipe
- HotDirtyWater: HotWater / Condenser–TFitPipe
- Water: Divert / Pipe
- FeedDirtyWater: HotWaterDiverter / BoilerPipe
- Sludge: Drainage / internalDrainPipe
- Residue: WastePipeline / WasteDrainagePipeline
- SafeDrinkingWater3: DistributionPipeline / CleanWaterDistributionPipeline
- BypassWater: BypassFeedPipe / ExcessWaterPipeline

### Not covered by the chain
- The PA chain "Distiller Operation" (39d5dc2b) has no upward realization. It covers Operate System, Provide Operator Interface and Monitor Operation, with Startup, Shutdown, Initialize and the lamp signals.
- The control loop (Processor on the Heat/Valve Controller, the Control Panel, Perform Routine Adjustments) is not in the Distill Water chain, so REQ-001211 doesn't reach it.

### Distribution-specific finding
Clean water leaves the distiller only through the Condenser's "c out" port, via DistributionPipeline / CleanWaterDistributionPipeline to the external Water Distribution System. Store, Distribute and Use Clean Water sit outside the system. A distribution test therefore exercises the Condenser output and that single link. Any test needing on-board storage, flow metering or a sampling point has no hardware in the PA to land on.
