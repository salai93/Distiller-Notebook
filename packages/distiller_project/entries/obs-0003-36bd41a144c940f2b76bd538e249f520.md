## Impact map: OA "Consume Safe Drinking Water" → Physical Architecture

**Model:** Distiller-Project @ master, commit 973e572. Traced OA activity → realizing SA function → LA → PA function → behavior PC → node PC.

| OA activity (OA reqs) | SA functions (SA reqs) | PA function → behavior PC → node PC |
|---|---|---|
| Purify Water (none) | Distill Water: Heat Water, Boil Water (DS.7), Condense Steam (DS.5, DS.8) | Heat Water + Condense Steam → Condenser → Condenser node; Boil Water → Evaporator → Mechanical Boiler; Divert Flow → Splitter → Tee Fitting; Throttle → Feed → Feed Valve (both in Diverter Assembly) |
| Manage Waste (MR.6) | Drain Residue (DS.9), Discharge Residue | Drain Residue → Drain → Drain Valve; Discharge Residue → not allocated inside the distiller |
| Operate System (MR.4.1, MR.8) | Operate System | PA Operate System not allocated inside the distiller; drives Provide Operator Interface → User Interface → Control Panel |
| Provide Operating Power (MR.1, MR.2) | Generate Heat (DS.1) | PA Generate Heat not allocated inside the distiller; Heat3 enters Evaporator via HeatSupply / Heat link (Heat Source → Mechanical Boiler) |
| Provide Dirty Water (MR.3) | Provide Dirty Water (DS.2) | not allocated inside the distiller; enters via SourcePipeline (Water Source → Condenser) |
| Store Water (none) | Store Clean Water (DS.3) | not allocated inside the distiller; SafeDrinkingWater3 rides DistributionPipeline (Condenser → Water Distribution System) |
| Distribute Water (MR.5) | Distribute Clean Water | not allocated inside the distiller |
| Use Water (none) | Use Clean Water | not allocated inside the distiller |

The control loop is also touched: Processor (on Heat/Valve Controller) hosts Monitor Operation and Perform Routine Adjustments. Through ThrottleControl, ResidueControl and BoilerControl over cables, these control Feed Valve, Drain Valve and Mechanical Boiler.

### Findings
- Purify Water and Manage Waste account for every internal Water Distiller component. Requirement changes on these two have the widest physical impact.
- Storage, distribution and use realize to no component inside the distiller. Any OA requirement change on Store/Distribute/Use Water lands on external actors, or means adding a new component such as an internal tank.
- The process itself (REQ-001211 Water Distribution Test) has no realizing functional chain in SA, LA or PA. Its trace runs only through the individual activities.
- Requirement coverage is uneven. Purify Water has none at OA but DS.5/7/8 at SA; Use Water has none at any layer.
- Not verified: whether the "not allocated inside the distiller" PA functions are allocated to the external actor components. The actor-node fabric did not show function allocation.
